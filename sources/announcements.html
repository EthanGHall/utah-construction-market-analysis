Building Permits Survey Announcements

  • 5/01/25 - Final annual estimates for 2024 of housing units authorized by building permits for the U.S. and by Census Region, Census Division, state, metropolitan area, county, permit-issuing place, and island areas are now available. Revised monthly estimates for the U.S. and Census Regions for the prior year which have been benchmarked to the final annual totals are also released. In addition, 2023 detailed data for Puerto Rico Municipios are now available in the 2023 island area file and data for total Puerto Rico is available in the 2023 state file. All other previously released 2023 data has not changed.

  • 2/26/25 - Notice of methodology change: With the January 2025 Monthly Building Permits Survey release on February 26, 2025, the Building Permits survey has made modifications to the methodology for imputation. The previous process calculated imputation factors by region and the new process will calculate factors at a mix of division and large individual states. For additional details on this change, see our imputation section of our methodology page.

  • 12/20/24 - Special Announcement: Due to the executive order issued by President Biden closing the federal government on December 24, 2024, the U.S. Census Bureau will release Revised Building Permits data on Monday, December 23, 2024. The original release date was scheduled for Tuesday, December 24. In the event of a lapse in appropriations, the Census Bureau will provide additional guidance on the release of principal federal economic indicator data and an updated release schedule as soon as it becomes available.

  • 11/26/24 - Notice of methodology change: With the January 2025 Monthly Building Permits Survey release on February 26, 2025, the Building Permits survey will make modifications to the methodology for imputation. The current process calculates imputation factors by region and the new process will calculate factors at a mix of division and large individual states. For additional details on this change, see our imputation section of our methodology page.

  • 5/01/24 - Final annual estimates for 2023 of housing units authorized by building permits for the U.S. and by Census Region, Census Division, state, metropolitan area, county, and permit-issuing place are now available. Revised monthly estimates for the U.S. and Census Regions for the prior year which have been "benchmarked" to the final annual totals are also released.

    Also, annual data for 2022 was also revised and re-released. This unscheduled revision was due to receiving additional late data and corrections to data for 2022 since initial publication that either impact the aggregate estimates or allow for additional geographic data to be published. The following files for 2022 was revised and replaced: State, MSA, County, and Place-level, along with a new file containing the geographic details for Island Areas.

  • 4/16/2024 - Special Announcement: With the release of the 2023 annual estimates of housing units authorized by building permits on May 1, 2024, the annual data for 2022 will also be revised and re-released. This unscheduled revision is due to receiving additional late data and corrections to data for 2022 since initial publication that either impact the aggregate estimates or allow for additional geographic data to be published.

    The two most notable corrections and additions for 2022 were:

    • New York City initially reported partial data for 2022 and 2023 and has since corrected the five Boroughs (Brooklyn, Bronx, Queens, Manhattan, and Staten Island) to contain full new residential building permit data.
    • Estimates for Puerto Rico will be released for the first time since 2016. Data for 2022 will also be available at the 78 municipios level, providing more geographic detail. While 2023 Puerto Rico data will not be available for the May 1, 2024 release, we anticipate releasing the same geographic data for 2023 as the data becomes available.

    The following files for 2022 will be revised and replaced on May 1, 2024: State, MSA, County, and Place-level, along with a new file containing the geographic details for Island Areas.

  • 2/26/24 - Notice: Revisions observed in the January 2024 Northeast totals are primarily attributable to a revised report from New York City. We are working with the local jurisdiction to evaluate the impact and incorporate any additional revisions for periods prior to January 2024 as necessary.

  • 1/25/24 - Beginning with the January 2024 monthly BPS data release, Micropolitan area data will be included in the existing Metropolitan data files. Data for Micropolitan areas will be in the same format and file as the existing Metropolitan data, with an additional field added to differentiate between a Metropolitan versus Micropolitan area. In addition, all files, titles, and references that currently only reference Metropolitan or MSA data will have their name updated to CBSA (Core Based Statistical Area), which encompasses both Metropolitan and Micropolitan areas.

    In addition, beginning with the January 2024 monthly release, the BPS data for CBSA's will incorporate any definitional, name or code changes according to the current Metropolitan and Micropolitan statistical area delineations that were announced by OMB effective July 2023. Please refer to: CBSA Changes for more information.

  • 5/01/23 - Final annual estimates for 2022 of housing units authorized by building permits for the U.S. and by Census Region, Census Division, state, metropolitan area, county, and permit-issuing place are now available. Revised monthly estimates for the U.S. and Census Regions for the prior year which have been "benchmarked" to the final annual totals are also released.

  • 2/24/23 - Notice of methodology change: With the release of January 2023 data, the Building Permits survey has changed its target universe from a 2014 basis to an annually updated universe. This change allows for more accurate and quicker coverage when jurisdictions change from not issuing building permits to issuing them. For additional details on this change and the impact on the New Residential Construction and Building Permits Survey data, see our  2023 Methodology Change FAQs

    The following changes are included in the January 2023 data releases. 126 jurisdictions where permit systems were discontinued since 2014 were removed from the survey data and 88 new jurisdictions that began issuing permits since 2014 were added to the survey data.

    In addition, several significant geography changes were processed and are included in the January 2023 data. These include the adoption of Connecticut’s nine Councils of Governments (COGs) as the county-equivalent geographic unit and the splitting of the Valdez-Cordova Census Area, Alaska to form Chugach Census Area and Copper River Census Area. For more information on these and other geography changes please see: Changes to Counties and County Equivalent Entities: 1970-Present (census.gov)

  • 11/17/22 - Notice of methodology change: Beginning with the January New Residential Construction release on February 16, 2023, the Building Permits survey will change its target universe from the current 2014 basis to an annually updated universe. This change will allow for more accurate and quicker coverage when jurisdictions change from not issuing building permits to issuing them. For additional details on this change and the impact on New Residential Construction, see our 2023 Methodology Change FAQs

  • 2/24/22 - Notice of methodology change: With the January 2022 data month, the monthly Building Permits Survey design has changed from a representative sample to a cut-off sample. This change allows complete local and county data on new housing units authorized by permits to be published monthly going forward. Initial summary level estimates for January 2022 were released with the New Residential Construction release on February 17, 2022. Complete local, County and State estimates were first released at www.census.gov/construction/bps/ on February 24, 2022. For additional details on this change and the impact on New Residential Construction, see our updated 2022 Methodology Change FAQs

  • 11/17/21 - Notice of methodology change: Beginning with the January 2022 New Residential Construction release on February 17, 2022, the monthly Building Permits Survey design will change from a representative sample to a cut-off sample. This change will allow complete local and county data on new housing units authorized by permits to be published on a monthly basis going forward. For additional details on this change and the impact on New Residential Construction, see our Methodology Change FAQs.
